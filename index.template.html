DOOZIE!

Last car on screen wins
Controllers: press A to join · B to leave · START to begin.
Click a car to switch it between Keyboard, Computer and Empty.
Stay on screen! Fall behind the camera or get shunted into the harbour and you're out. Last car left wins the round (+2), second gets +1, first out loses 1. First to the target wins.
Each player has a boost. Esc pauses. G switches Fancy / Simple graphics.
Mouse rockets: anyone can click on the track to fire a rocket. Click near a car and it locks on.
