How to play

Stay on screen! Fall behind the camera or get shunted off the edge and you're out. Last car left wins the round (+2), second gets +1, first out loses 1.

Drive through ? crates to grab a weapon and press FIRE to use it. Everyone has a boost.

Keyboard: Red W A S D, boost Q, fire E · Blue arrows, boost Right Shift, fire / · Green I J K L, boost U, fire O · Gold T F G H, boost R, fire Y.

Controllers: stick or D-pad steers, RT or A accelerates, LT or B brakes, X or RB fires, Y or LB boosts, START pauses.

Mouse rockets: anyone can click the track to fire a rocket; click near a car and it locks on.

Phones: scan the code to drive or to watch and mess with the players. Laptops: press Join a game and type the room code.

Keys: Esc pause · V graphics · C camera · N frame rate · B / Z call Bigfoot / the Ghost car.
